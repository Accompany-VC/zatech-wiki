# #beginners

<details open>
<summary><strong>Purpose</strong></summary>

A welcoming and supportive channel for anyone in the South African tech community to ask any question about technology without fear of judgment. Whether you're just starting your tech journey, switching careers, or simply need help understanding a concept, this is a safe space where no question is too simple or too basic. Experienced community members are here to help guide and mentor newcomers with patience and encouragement.
