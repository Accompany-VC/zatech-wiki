### How big a system do I need to power my devices?

**Option A:** Measure actual startup peaks and average usage.  
**Option B:** Use equipment specs to estimate load and storage needs.

### What is kWh, Ah, and kVA?

- **A** = Current draw  
- **kVA** ≈ Watts (Amps × Volts ÷ 1000)  
- **W** = Power (V × A)  
- **Ah** = Battery capacity (100Ah @ 12V ≈ 100A for 1 hour)  
- **kWh** = Energy consumed in 1 hour

### How do I calculate storage needs?

1. Estimate watts consumed per hour  
2. Multiply by expected outage hours  
3. Compare kVA (output) vs kWh (storage)  
4. Size UPS, batteries, and solar accordingly
