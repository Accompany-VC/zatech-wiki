# #mentoring

> Connect mentors and mentees to accelerate growth in the South African tech community.

**693 members** • **Created: October 10, 2016**

---

<details open>
<summary><strong>Purpose</strong></summary>

A dedicated space for South African tech professionals to find mentors or become mentors themselves. Mentoring accelerates growth for both the mentee and the mentor, creating a culture of continuous learning and knowledge sharing. Whether you're seeking long-term mentorship, need guidance on a specific problem, want a quick code review, or are ready to share your expertise with others, this channel helps facilitate meaningful mentoring relationships across all experience levels.
