<summary><strong>Purpose</strong></summary>

A dedicated channel for community members to buy and sell second-hand items directly to each other. Whether you're upgrading your tech gear or looking for a good deal, this is the place for peer-to-peer sales. Remember to mention your location when posting, and keep it personal - no commercial activity or business sales allowed.
